## Blog — small-business marketing insights (Malaysia)
- [SEO isn't enough anymore — in 2026 you also need GEO so AI can find you (做 SEO 还不够，2026 还要 GEO 让 AI 搜得到你)](https://www.h2o-dreamer-studio.com/blog/seo-vs-geo-ai-search.html): The difference between SEO and GEO (Generative Engine Optimization) for a Malaysian small business. SEO gets you found in Google's list of links (fight for ranking and clicks); GEO gets you named inside the answer that AI engines — ChatGPT, Perplexity, Google AI Overviews — write (fight to be quoted as the source). In 2026 more customers ask AI instead of scrolling Google and stop at the AI answer without clicking any link (zero-click), so being left out of the AI answer is a silent, invisible loss. GEO in plain terms is five things done at build time: answer-first content (a liftable direct answer at the top of every page), structured data with a real named author, concrete quotable facts ({{starter}}, 2026, Malaysia — not vague claims), never burying prices/services inside images (AI reads text, not posters), and letting AI crawlers in via robots.txt + sitemap + llms.txt. For most small businesses this is a one-time foundation built into the website, not a monthly SEO retainer (which only makes sense for big players fighting over the most competitive keywords). Includes a 3-question self-check for whether ChatGPT/Perplexity can currently find your site.
- [DIY on Wix, or hire someone? Also covers when Wix genuinely is the right choice (still testing an idea, or budget is truly zero), plus a 3-question test to decide which fits you.
