What should a Johor Bahru business website have?

A JB website should answer practical questions fast. Many customers drive in from Singapore or from the other side of town, and they want to know price, place and how to book before they message you.

💱

Prices in RM and SGD

If Singapore customers cross over, show both currencies. They should not have to convert in their head.

🗺️

Waze and Google Maps

Put both buttons side by side, plus simple directions, like how far you are from the CIQ checkpoint.

💬

One-tap WhatsApp

Most people here would rather WhatsApp than fill in a form. A pre-filled message makes the first step easy.

🗣️

Talk to me in English or 中文

I work in both. Explain your business the way you normally would, and I will turn it into a clear website.
