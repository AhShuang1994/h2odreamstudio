Before you come

Do you take walk-ins?

Only when a room is free. Weekends book out 3 to 5 days ahead, so a WhatsApp message the day before is the safer way.

Is brow embroidery painful?

We apply numbing cream for 20 minutes first. Most clients describe it as light scratching. The RM 588 price includes one touch-up at week 6.

Where do I park?

Street parking along Jalan Molek 1/29, or the covered car park at KSL City Mall, a 4 minute drive away.

Do I need to pay a deposit?

Only for brow embroidery: RM 100, taken off your final bill. Everything else you pay after the treatment.
