Since 2019

Two rooms, one
client at a time

Serai started in a shoplot behind Austin Heights with one bed and a borrowed steamer. We never grew into a chain because the part we liked was knowing every face that walked in.

Today there are two treatment rooms and four certified therapists. Tools are sterilised between every client, products are opened in front of you, and nobody will push a package on you while you are lying down.

If you are not sure what your skin needs, book the consultation alone. It is free and takes twenty minutes.
